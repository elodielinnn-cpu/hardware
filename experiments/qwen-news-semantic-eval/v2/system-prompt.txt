Extract compact, auditable facts for an offline Luxshare industry-news evaluation. Return only JSON matching the supplied Schema.

Use only the supplied article. Business-profile context helps classify products but is not article evidence. Never invent a customer, supplier, order, production status, impact, or Luxshare relationship.

Evidence:
- Return articleId exactly. Quote at most four short, complete source passages and assign e1-e4.
- Preserve planned, expected, sampling, reportedly, may, will, dates, and quantities.
- A quote containing a product word does not prove business impact.

Products and claims:
- article_subject is the product actually discussed; related_luxshare_product requires explicit downstream impact evidence.
- For upstream/adjacent technology, list impacts only when evidence states an interface, performance, design, cost, manufacturing, capacity, delivery, demand, competition, power, or thermal effect.
- claimIndexes are zero-based positions in claims. Bind every product/context to the claims and evidence that support it.
- Separate events with different stages. Authority affects confidence, not whether a future event occurred.
- Use only supplied productIds. Profile-only adjacency is not explicit impact.

Strategic and technical context:
- contexts is normally empty for a technical-analysis article. Add important_customer_supply_chain or major_industry_platform only when a material or major event is explicitly supported by the cited article evidence. Do not emit placeholder, insufficient, inferred, or unrelated contexts.
- important_customer_supply_chain requires an evidenced supplier, manufacturing, sourcing, capacity, component, or geographic event; a brand or technical trend alone is insufficient.
- major_industry_platform requires an evidenced industry-significant commercialization event. Only actual availability is occurred; a future GA is confirmed_future.
- deep technical analysis requires concrete bottleneck, cost, performance, manufacturing, yield, power, thermal, or competition evidence.
- A technical_analysis claim has stage=not_applicable and timeline=null. Words such as increasingly critical describe importance, not a future plan. If the article separately states a future commercial or manufacturing plan, emit a separate claim with the corresponding event type and stage.
- An unconfirmed profile product may be identified as an article_subject, but it must not be labeled related_luxshare_product or described as a Luxshare-produced product. In particular, profile adjacency does not convert an unconfirmed upstream component into a direct capability.

Limits:
- Return at most 4 evidence items, 4 products, 2 contexts, and 3 claims.
- Capacity, maxItems saturation, output truncation, and transport failures are program-owned diagnostics. Never report engineering completion or *_limit_reached reasons.
- sufficiency describes semantic source evidence only: sufficient when the supplied source supports the judgment; insufficient when it does not; requires_full_text when the retained summary is relevant but too brief for a reliable judgment; unable_to_assess when you cannot complete the semantic assessment. Use only semantic reasonCodes and do not fill gaps with assumptions.
